# Phase 11 Sequence

Phase 11 is internally linear. Exactly one Phase 11 subphase may be active at a
time. A successor subphase may not enter implementation until its predecessor
has an accepted and merged closure record.

- 11A — Communications Domain Architecture and Governance
- 11B — Communications Data Model
- 11C — Orchestration Runtime
- 11D — Telephony Adapter (Sandbox)
- 11E — Receptionist Runtime
- 11F — Security, Consent & Trust
- 11G — Observability & Operations
- 11H — Staging Validation
- 11I — Communications Production Activation

## Delivery Rule

Production activation is not part of 11A through 11H.

11I remains a separately authorized Communications production activation gate.
It does not authorize broader platform production activation outside the
Communications Completion milestone.

Phase 12 planning remains locked until all Phase 11 subphases and formal Phase
11 closure are complete.
