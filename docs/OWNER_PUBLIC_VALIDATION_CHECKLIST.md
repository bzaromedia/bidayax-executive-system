# Owner Public Validation Checklist

Use this checklist after redeployment to validate The Executive Card on real devices.

## Cards

- A.D Garner card opens at `https://theexecutivecard.online/card/ad-garner`.
- Naimah J. Barnes card opens at `https://theexecutivecard.online/card/naimah-barnes`.
- Sean Hall card opens at `https://theexecutivecard.online/card/sean-hall`.
- QR, Download, and Share remain visible in the fixed bottom dock.
- No bottom dock overlap appears on iPhone Safari, Android Chrome, desktop Chrome, desktop Edge, and desktop Firefox.
- No horizontal drift appears while scrolling.
- The splash shows the logo briefly and then clears.

## Receptionist

- Open Receptionist launches a compact sheet.
- Required consent is enforced.
- English, Spanish, Arabic, French, Mandarin, Urdu, and Hindi are selectable.
- Schedule meeting, route message, request callback, qualify lead, general inquiry, partnership request, and support request are selectable.
- A normal request is accepted and queued.
- A malicious instruction override request is blocked or safely rejected.
- Dashboard queue shows request ID, executive, visitor, company, language, request type, status, urgency, provider status, and audit timeline.

## Provider Boundary

- Live telephony remains disabled unless provider credentials and safety flags are configured and tested.
- Email and calendar dispatch remain internal-queue mode unless provider credentials and dispatch flags are configured and tested.
