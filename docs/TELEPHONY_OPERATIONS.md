# Telephony Operations

Operational state in Phase 6:

- Providers are not configured.
- Calling is disabled.
- Recording and transcription are disabled.
- The dashboard exposes foundation readiness, not live call controls.
- Usage cost rates are zero until future provider contracts and billing policy are approved.

Before future production activation, operations must add provider credentials, webhook validation, rate limits, spend limits, kill switches, alerting, retention settings, and jurisdiction-specific consent review.
