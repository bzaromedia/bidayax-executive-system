## Caution

This document is hypothetical positioning only. It is not investment advice, a fundraising claim, a guarantee, an appraisal, or evidence of investor validation.

Any valuation depends on implementation quality, pilots, customers, revenue, defensibility, retention, enterprise adoption, competitive dynamics, and market timing.

| Scenario | Hypothetical range | Conditions |

## Required Guardrails

- Do not present valuation as guaranteed.
- Do not present this document as investment advice.
- Do not claim investor validation unless evidence exists.
- Do not use valuation ranges in public marketing as a promise of business outcome.
- Tie future valuation discussion to evidence: pilots, customers, revenue, retention, defensibility, and market adoption.

## Current Evidence Boundary

The current repository evidence supports product and implementation readiness work, not investor validation or market valuation. Commercial valuation language must remain cautious until customer and revenue evidence exists.
