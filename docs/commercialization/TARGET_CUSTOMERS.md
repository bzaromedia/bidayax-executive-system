# Target Customers

Project: The Executive Card™  
Owner: BidayaX LLC  
Public URL: https://theexecutivecard.online

The Executive Card™ is positioned for organizations where executive identity, relationship intelligence, and follow-up discipline affect revenue, trust, or strategic access.

## 1. Enterprise Executives

- CEOs
- founders
- presidents
- managing directors
- board members
- investment executives

Primary value: turn executive identity, introductions, QR engagement, meetings, and follow-up into structured relationship intelligence.

## 2. Professional Services

- law firms
- accounting firms
- consulting firms
- wealth management
- commercial insurance
- executive recruiters

Primary value: preserve relationship context across high-value meetings, referrals, calls, and follow-up workflows.

## 3. Commercial Real Estate

- brokerages
- developers
- property investment firms

Primary value: capture investor, tenant, partner, and referral engagement from events, site visits, and executive introductions.

## 4. Healthcare Organizations

- hospital systems
- medical groups
- private practices
- healthcare consultants

Primary value: create professional executive identity surfaces and route business inquiries without implying clinical, medical, or regulated-care automation.

## 5. Financial Services

- banks
- private equity
- venture capital
- family offices
- investment advisors

Primary value: organize executive relationship intelligence from investor, founder, advisor, and partner interactions.

## 6. Enterprise Sales Teams

- SaaS
- cybersecurity
- manufacturing
- telecommunications

Primary value: give senior sellers and executives a controlled identity surface that captures high-intent engagement signals for follow-up.

## 7. Government & Public Sector

- municipalities
- agencies
- economic development organizations

Primary value: provide professional identity and inquiry-routing foundations for public-facing leadership and development teams.

## 8. White-Label Partners

- digital agencies
- branding firms
- CRM consultants
- IT service providers
- systems integrators

Primary value: future channel opportunity for delivering executive identity intelligence capabilities under partner-led implementation models.

## v1.0 Commercial Boundary

The v1.0 release includes implemented, tested, production-buildable card, interaction, dashboard, scoring, graph, telemetry, receptionist-workflow foundation, telephony-safety foundation, and human-approved improvement foundation capabilities. Deferred enterprise trust layers are not active product capabilities in v1.0.
